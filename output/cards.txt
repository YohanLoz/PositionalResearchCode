% Card 1 The Malsuiilu Ithardania is commonly called the Itherstan Velari. It is a semi-aquatic mammal known for unusually flexible social behavior. %
---
% Card 2 Adult Itherstan Velari typically measure between 1.4 and 1.9 meters long, including the tail. Their bodies are low-slung and built for moving through reeds, mud, and shallow water. %
---
% Card 3 The species has four webbed limbs, but its front limbs retain dexterous, fingerlike claws. These claws are used for opening shells, knotting plant fibers, and grooming companions. %

% Card 4 Itherstan Velari fur is naturally dark at the roots and pale at the tips. When wet, this layered coloration produces a rippling silver effect. %

% Card 5 Each Velari has a unique pattern of whisker spots around its muzzle. Individuals can recognize one another from these markings at surprisingly long distances. %

% Card 7 Itherstan Velari ears can fold tightly against the skull. This protects them from silt during underwater dives. %
---
% Card 8 A Velari can remain submerged for nearly twelve minutes when resting. During active pursuit, it usually resurfaces much sooner. %

% Card 9 The species breathes through lungs but can absorb limited oxygen through specialized skin on its throat. This adaptation helps it remain still beneath floating vegetation. %

% Card 10 Itherstan Velari nostrils close automatically underwater. Their noses also contain sensitive folds that detect chemical traces in currents. %
---
% Card 11 The Velari’s tail functions as both a rudder and a signaling device. A slow side-to-side sweep generally indicates calmness, while rapid tapping may warn of danger. %

% Card 12 Its hind feet contain elongated toes connected by elastic membranes. These membranes stretch during swimming and contract during climbing. %

% Card 13 Itherstan Velari teeth continue growing throughout life. Hard-shelled prey and mineral-rich reeds naturally keep them worn down. %

% Card 19 Itherstan Velari sometimes share meals with unrelated individuals. Food sharing is most common after successful cooperative hunts. %
